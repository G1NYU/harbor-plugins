SOURCE NOTES & KNOWN ISSUES 03 / 03
  • !MangaDex (12 Nov 2026): temporarily removed from Main after a reported full failure in Harbor. An automated request to its API returned a browser-verification page instead of manga data, although MangaDex's own status page reports the API operational. The exact Harbor failure path still needs isolation; the converter and source remain preserved for repair and retesting.
  • +New (11 Nov 2026): Read Comics Online, OceComic and LatestComics EN bring Western comics into Main. 🇫🇷 TooComic FR has joined Main too, covering French manga, manhwa and comics. All four were tested directly in Harbor.
  • iFrench chapters: TooComic FR v1.2.2 now returns chapters and cover art in Harbor. Large chapter lists can still be slow; the source is marked French, so chapter language should be French, not English.
  • !After updating a plugin: use the extension's Update button, toggle it off and back on to restart Harbor's cached worker, then clear Settings → Storage → Caches → Manga browse cache if covers, titles or chapters still look stale. This does not clear reading history.
  • iLanguage tags: they reflect each plugin's declared language, not a promise that every series or chapter is available in that language. “Multilingual” sources may include several languages.
  • !Experimental sources: BatCave is parked in the separate Testing repo, not Main. Its Cloudflare challenge can block reader requests in Harbor. The legacy Extended catalogue currently contains the same sources as Main.
  • !Legion Scans: browsing is limited to the first page (around 31 series); search and full history have not been verified in Harbor.
  • !Atsumaru artwork: some catalogue covers may be blank even when series pages and readers load. Some VortexScans and MangaDemon chapters come from third-party sites and can fail individually.
  • ieBook alpha: eBook sources have different reading capabilities. Library Genesis Stream is download-only; availability and reader support vary by provider.
  • ×Not in Main: Kagane (Cloudflare), Mangasushi and MangaBuddy (image Referer requirements); MangaFire, MangaTown and Mangapill were removed from the curated catalogue.
